Context - future plans:

I am planning on skinning this app with proper graphics to look like real hardware.
With that in mind, ideally all windows should look good at the same scale.

Plan is to go old hardware (lab oscilloscope, with knobs and digital readouts for text fields/values), with signs of wear.

Rec panel and Save panel will have similar central big buttons that do the main thing (Record and Export).  
Parts will be labelled to simulate the old plastic Dymo sticky labels.
Tickboxes/toggles will be in the form of flip switches
Selectors will be knobs


For the moment I'd like to remove the dots in the background on all 3 panels, it's a bit distracting at this stage.
