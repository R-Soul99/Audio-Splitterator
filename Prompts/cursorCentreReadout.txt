## 1. Move dynamic cursor readout

The dynamic time/readout currently displayed near the top of the waveform cursor can still interfere visually with fade handles and waveform editing.

Move this readout so it is **vertically centred on the cursor, in the gap between the left and right waveform channels**.

Requirements:

- Keep it horizontally attached to/following the cursor as it does now.
- Position it in the central gap between the L/R channels.
- It should not obscure either waveform unnecessarily.
- The readout itself must not capture pointer/mouse events (`pointer-events: none` or equivalent).
- Do not change the cursor's existing time calculation or behaviour.
- Do not change fade-handle behaviour.

This is an initial UI experiment; do not create a separate permanent readout elsewhere in the panel.

## 2. Investigate/fix selection near the end of the waveform

It is currently noticeably difficult to create or extend a selection over the **trailing/end portion of the waveform**.

Do not assume the cause before inspecting the implementation.

In particular, investigate:

- pointer hit-testing near the right-hand edge;
- selection drag/clamping calculations;
- waveform width versus drawable/audio width;
- any overlays positioned over the waveform;
- the vertical zoom controls and their container/hit area;
- whether controls or transparent elements are intercepting pointer events;
- behaviour when dragging beyond the final audio pixel/right boundary.

The vertical zoom controls are suspected because they occupy this area, but confirm the actual cause.

### Desired behaviour

Selecting the final part of a recording should feel exactly as easy as selecting audio elsewhere.

The user must be able to:

- start a selection near the end;
- drag an existing selection towards the end;
- select all the way to the final sample/end of the recording;
- reach the end even if the pointer moves slightly beyond the waveform while dragging.

Waveform controls must not create an unexpected dead zone that prevents selection.

Do not break the vertical zoom controls themselves.
